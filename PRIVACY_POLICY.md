# Privacy Policy – Focus Track

**Effective Date:** 23 November 2025  
**Last Updated:** 24 December 2025

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Focus Track ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Focus Track collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

Focus Track is designed to help users improve productivity by tracking their app and screen activity locally on their device and displaying personalized insights. No productivity or activity data is uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any third party, including the developer.

By using Focus Track, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Local Productivity Data (Stored Only on Your Device)

Focus Track collects the following activity data locally on your device:

- Active application/window usage time
- Application names (visible locally only)
- Total focus duration
- Idle time (if enabled)
- Basic system interaction history relevant to focus tracking

This data is stored only on your device and is used solely to display productivity insights inside the App.

### B. Crash & Diagnostic Data (Limited, Automatic)

To improve app stability and performance, Focus Track may automatically collect crash and diagnostic reports when the App encounters an unexpected error.

Crash reports may include:

- App version and build information
- Operating system version
- Device and system configuration
- Error logs and stack traces related to the crash

Crash reports do not include:

- Personal identity information
- Application usage or productivity data
- Keystrokes, content, or screenshots

Crash and diagnostic data is sent directly to a secure server and is used only for debugging, performance monitoring, and improving app reliability.

### C. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

- Personal identity information (name, email, location)
- Financial, health, or contact data
- Keystrokes or sensitive user input
- Any cloud-based productivity or activity data

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- All productivity and activity data is stored locally on your device.
- Productivity and activity data is not transmitted over the internet or to any external servers.
- The developer cannot access any of your locally stored information.
- Data is only used to display your productivity analytics inside the App.
- For better performance, some data may be stored temporarily in local system memory or local application data folders.

## 4. Permissions

Focus Track may require system permissions such as:

- Activity monitoring access (to detect active apps/windows)
- Notification permissions (for reminders and productivity tips)

These permissions are used strictly to provide app functionality and are not used for tracking any personal identity.

## 5. Third-Party Sharing

- We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer productivity or activity data to any third-party services or individuals.
- No advertising or analytics services are integrated.
- The App operates primarily offline, except for limited crash diagnostics.

## 6. Data Control & Deletion

You have full control over your data:

- You can clear activity history within the App settings.
- Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Focus Track is not intended for users under the age of 13. No personal data is knowingly collected from children.

## 8. Microsoft Store Compliance

- No hidden tracking or analytics.
